# Golmelo Product Marketing Context

## Positioning

Golmelo is an online atelier for handmade fabric flowers. It serves three connected needs: ready-to-order flowers, fully customized orders, and online fabric-flower education.

## Primary Goals

1. Increase orders for ready and customized products.
2. Increase qualified requests for online courses.

## Audiences

- Personal buyers looking for a fabric-flower accessory for clothing, hats, hair, or gifts.
- Tailors and maisons looking for distinctive finishing pieces.
- Beginner through professional learners interested in fabric-flower making.

Most product buyers do not arrive with an exact model in mind. The catalog should give them ideas, then help them request a suitable customized result.

## Brand

- Luxury, delicate, feminine, editorial, and uncluttered.
- Never girlish, noisy, or visually similar to a crowded marketplace.
- Preserve the current navigation, landing-page section order, image-led product grid, and overall visual language.

## Differentiators

- More than a decade of practical experience.
- Consultation before an order is submitted.
- Full customization by color, size, fabric, flower type, attachment, count, composition, and use.
- Catalog photography comes from real customer orders.
- Online courses distill the atelier's experience into a clear, concise learning path.

## Commercial Truths

- Current catalog items are real products and have a base price supplied by the site administrator.
- Customization can change the final price.
- Prices, stock, customer names, and partner names must never be invented.
- Online payment is currently outside scope.
- The creator's face and personal story are not part of the current brand presentation.

## Measurement

Primary conversions are submitted product/custom orders and submitted course requests. Signups and contact submissions are secondary conversions. Analytics must never receive phone numbers, names, free-text messages, addresses, or order notes.
